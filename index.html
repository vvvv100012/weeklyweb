Ceasefire Relief, Cooling Oil Put Crypto Back on Firmer Ground
Crypto found a firmer footing this week as ceasefire progress between the U.S. and Iran, along with signs that transit through the Strait of Hormuz could normalize, pulled oil lower and brought risk appetite back into the market. BTC rebounded as headlines improved, implied volatility cooled, and short covering added fuel to the move. We believe this is still more of a relief rally inside a macro-sensitive range than the start of a clean breakout, but the tape can keep improving if oil stays contained and spot demand continues to absorb shocks. Attention now turns to the April 10 negotiations between Iran and the United States. Until that event passes, the latest strength may still be used by parts of the market to trim exposure at better levels, rather than as proof that the macro overhang has fully cleared.
